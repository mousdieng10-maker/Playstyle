Playstyle

A Real-Life RPG & Statistics App Playstyle is a gamified self-improvement app that transforms personal growth into a football (FIFA Ultimate Team) inspired experience. Instead of measuring football ability, Playstyle measures real-life attributes. Every user receives a personalized Playstyle Card upon creating an account, visually inspired by FIFA Ultimate Team player cards. This card represents the user's current "real-life stats" and evolves over time as they complete quests and improve themselves.

Core Statistics Each user has five primary attributes, rated on a scale similar to FIFA player ratings (e.g. 1–99):

Pace – Speed of completing tasks, reacting to situations, and maintaining momentum.

Acceleration – Ability to get started quickly, build habits, and overcome procrastination.

Physical – Fitness, health, endurance, strength, sleep, and overall physical wellbeing.

Meta (Intelligence) – Learning ability, problem-solving, strategy, and knowledge acquisition.

Vision – Planning, creativity, long-term thinking, decision making, and awareness. An overall rating (OVR) is calculated from these five stats, just like a FIFA player card.

Core Features

Upon signing up, users get to choose a player playstyle that will define what kind of quests they get:

Tekkers: Gives tech oriented quests.

All Rounder: Gives tech, reading, fitness oriented quests.

Playmaker: Gives reading oriented quests.

Defender: Gives fitness oriented quests. 

Quests & Achievements Users log real-life quests and achievements, which directly weigh into their five stats. Completing a quest increases stats and contributes to your overall OVR.

Streaks Consistent logging builds a streak, rewarding regular engagement with the app the same way real form/momentum works in football.

Decay Stats gradually decay if quests aren't logged for an extended period, keeping the card a living reflection of your current habits rather than a permanent record of past effort.

Card Tiers As your OVR grows, your Playstyle Card visually upgrades through tiers (Bronze → gold → diamond → ruby), mirroring FIFA's card rarity system.

Match Day Your card is tested against randomly generated opponent cards. The result reflects your current stats, a strong, well-rounded card performs better. This gives stat growth real consequence.
